## Repair order and acceptance

1. Payment integrity: use one idempotent order-to-sale transaction/service for all confirmed payment paths; deterministic sale IDs, explicit linkage, validated state transitions, stock changes and immutable payment references. Until an authenticated bank/provider result is integrated, an uploaded slip must remain awaiting owner verification rather than automatically paid. Never equate amount/QR readability with settlement.
2. Cash and debt: introduce dated, immutable money movements for receipts, debt collections, refunds and drawer adjustments; use integer minor currency units, method, actor, source and session links. Enforce a single active session transactionally and immutable close boundaries. Compute expected cash from actual cash movements, not a mutable sale flag. Do not create a second sale when collecting existing debt.
3. Refund/cancellation and reports: separate unpaid cancellation, paid refund and stock return/waste; maintain links/audit history and reverse loyalty consistently. Share calculation rules between dashboard, reports, exports and AI; distinguish booked revenue, cash received, outstanding debt and gross profit. Include modifier costs and disclose unknown costs.
4. Historical reconciliation: first run a read-only report of paid orders missing sales, duplicate order-linked sales, overlapping sessions and late offline movements. Review discrepancies before writing compensating entries; never silently rewrite old closed reports or delete evidence.

Acceptance scenarios: same-shift and next-day refunds; cash versus transfer refund; partial debt payments on a later day and from two terminals; full/partial refund after debt collection; webhook duplicate/retry after each write boundary; cancel racing with payment confirmation; offline sale syncing after close; two simultaneous session opens/closes; modifiers/discount/service charge; ingredient handlers arriving in either order; failure at each step must not duplicate sale, cash movement, stock or loyalty effects.

No fixes or release were performed during the initial audit above. The implementation status below supersedes that initial status; original line references describe the pre-fix code.

## Implemented follow-up — 2026-10-05, source only

- Confirmed online payments now create one deterministic, linked sale, stock change and money movement in a transaction. Repeated confirmation and Stripe webhook delivery do not create another sale. Unpaid cancellation requires a reason; paid cancellation goes through refund handling.
- Customer-uploaded order slips remain awaiting owner review. Reading a QR amount and matching a bank notification no longer constitute payment confirmation. Stripe completion must report a paid THB payment with the expected amount. Subscription checkout events also use a transaction receipt to prevent duplicate renewal on repeated/completed/async events.
- Immutable money movements distinguish sales, debt collections and refunds, with integer satang values, payment method, actor, timestamps and cash-session linkage. Debt collection preserves history, checks the current balance and does not create another sale.
- Only one cash session can open. Close captures an immutable server summary; concurrent/repeated close cannot overwrite it. The confirmation screen and Z-report use the saved count, including when another terminal closed first. Sales, net money received and change in debt have separate labels.
- Cross-day refunds and debt collections affect the current cash movement period. Already-closed snapshots remain unchanged. Late/offline movements without a valid session are flagged for reconciliation; local pending checkouts/offline receipts block close on that device. Other devices still need to sync before closing.
- Legacy sessions can be archived with an explicitly acknowledged actual cash count and a reconciliation flag, without inventing an expected cash balance. Historical financial records are not silently repaired.
- Refunds preserve debt/collection history, refund only collected money for credit sales, cancel remaining debt, update linked orders and reverse recorded loyalty awards once. Stripe pending refunds do not finalize the sale or stock until succeeded. Stock return defaults to false and requires an explicit choice; previously consumed ingredients are handled idempotently regardless of event order.
- Cancelling sent/prepared table items records inventory waste and consumes relevant stock/ingredients unless the owner explicitly indicates preparation had not started. Retail/table sale stock checks now agree; recipe products avoid double deduction of both product stock and ingredients.
- Dashboard/report/AI financial totals use recorded sales consistently. Modifier cost snapshots and proportional discount allocation feed product revenue/gross profit; unknown costs are identified. Paid orders missing a sale are review findings rather than invented revenue.
- Added owner-only money-history and bounded, read-only reconciliation views (up to 200 paid orders, 200 unassigned movements, 100 recent sessions, with a truncation notice). Server rules prevent direct client edits to finalized financial records and staff access to privileged actions.
