## Integration and provenance

- Downloaded WebP pixels remain unchanged. The app launch artwork sits behind
  the transparent screen aperture in HTML/CSS. No generative repainting or screen
  image editing was used.
- The tablet frame is rotated 90 degrees in CSS for landscape presentation; the
  Pokpok logo remains upright. Each outer device retains the image's aspect ratio.
- The real kiosk chassis replaces the drawn pedestal and fake slot. Its visible
  card reader is part of the supplier image, not a claim of Pokpok payment-device
  integration. Kiosk self-ordering remains a future concept in the hero copy.
- Existing tap, swipe, mouse drag, keyboard and reduced-motion behavior remains.
  No separate selector or explanatory text is added to the device screens.
